Read your rowing, stroke by stroke

Load a recording from an M5Tab Rowing monitor. Every stroke is found again and shown as drive, finish, run and approach, with the same hint the device gives on the water.

  1. Stop the session on the tablet.
  2. Plug it into this computer with USB-C and press Read from Tab5, or open the recording files you copied off the card (select the _imu.csv together with the _seconds.csv, _laps.csv and _info.csv of that session).
  3. Pick the session, step through the strokes, and switch the chart to the whole session or by rate.

Recordings are analysed on this server and are not kept: they are gone when it restarts. Reading from the tablet needs Chrome or Edge.

Do not have the device yet?

It is an open project. You need an M5Stack Tab5, a GNSS unit and a microSD card. The firmware installs from your browser, no tools needed.

  1. Get the parts: M5Stack Tab5, M5Stack Unit GNSS on Port A, and a FAT32 microSD card.
  2. Open the install page in Chrome or Edge, plug in the Tab5 and press install.
  3. Mount it rigidly in the boat, row, and come back here with the recording.
